The Problem Company x Common House

August 2024, posted by Andreea Bunica

events

We're announcing a new partnership with Common House! A project that is very dear to us, a community for budding creative entrepreneurs. Starting in August, we're starting a series of monthly events named "The Bubble": a place to laugh and cry, vent, and be completely honest and unfiltered about our businesses.

Find our more about The Bubble

September to December 2024 will be the first season of The Bubble, and it will feature monthly meet-up events and a series of masterclasses meant to help founders fix the most frequent issues we've heard in previous editions of our community meet-ups. You can find all the news, monthly announcements, as well as a calendar for the events we're organising related to The Bubble on our Instagram and Linkedin accounts, at @theproblem.company
