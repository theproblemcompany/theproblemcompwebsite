Factory Floor Optimisation

Tags

Hands-on

Online/Offline

Complex

This is a targeted service most suitable for medium to large factories that are looking to optimise their floor layout. The goal of this service is to computationally analyse visual and spatial data we collect from your factory, in order to determine the current usage and occupation of the space, map out redundancies and identify better paths for your space. We output recommendations for work stations, machinery and storage placements, floor pathways and overall factory layout options that best fulfill your business goals.

This service is a primer to working with a robotics integrator for digitising or building a smart factory. Our work ends with making sure that we coordinate with your integrator of choice and we advise them on best space layout practices we've discovered.

Process> > >

Here's the project phases we go through:

1. Record visual data and map the physical factory space

2. Apply computer vision algorithms to extract knowledge

3. Perform spatial simulations for optimal factory floor layouts

4. Collaborate with robotic solutions integrators
