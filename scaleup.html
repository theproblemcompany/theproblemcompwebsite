Fancy seeing you here! In this short but mighty 30 minute webinar we'll unpack the basic concepts of creative automation and pin down the first steps you can take for your business. This is designed for ambitious manufacturing, design and physical services entrepreneurs looking to expand their companies by doing more with... less- way less.

Here at the problem company, our goal is lowering the threshold to advanced technology so that amazing people like you can use it to change the world. We focus on companies with unique processes that require elegant, sensible technology capable of mimimcking human craft and feeling. This webinar is meant to structure how you think about automation and how you identify and deploy solutions for the problems you are facing.

A brief overview of the schedule:

Well, first: a non-nonsense approach to technology. We'll deliver an applied framework for dealing with technology. Expect:

> A short overview of advanced technology and systems available

> Tools to identify what and when you need automation in your business

> A short overview of what an automation process is, and how to work with your employees to make it happen

The event is free. We can't wait to see you there!
