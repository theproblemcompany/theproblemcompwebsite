Laservalley Innovation Bootcamp

June 2024, posted by Andreea Bunica

media

The first place we ever spoke about The Problem Company publicly. Our founder, Andreea, described the goals and the mission of the company, during the final pitching session of the Laservalley Innovation Bootcamp.

Watch the full pitch here

We spent three intensive days in the Magurele Science-Park organised Bootcamp, looking for answers and solutions to our most burning question: how can we make advanced technology accessible outside of big research centers? We've benefitted from long chats with mentors, and a lot of brainstorming later, we even came up with a concept and directions for where the company should deposit its efforts to next.
