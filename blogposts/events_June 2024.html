Creative Automation Workshop in Bucharest

June 2024, posted by Andreea Bunica

events

We kicked off our in-person workshops with a small gathering held at Common House, in June. We invited a handful of ambitious entrepreneurs to discuss automation, technology, and most importantly, the practicalities and benefits of using advanced tech solutions.

Watch the workshop recap video

Our founder, Andreea, spoke about spotting inefficiencies in your business process, and how to identify whether your problems can be solved using technology or not. The workshop included a hands-on session of mapping an overview of company's processes and figuring out where the repetitive or inefficient processes happen.
